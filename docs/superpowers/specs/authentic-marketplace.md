# PojokPixel: Authentic Marketplace Direction

## Goal

Make PojokPixel feel like a specific Indonesian marketplace for independent digital creators by presenting useful product details honestly and removing unsupported social proof and generic promotional language.

## Audience and product job

Indonesian buyers browsing practical design and web resources, and creators publishing them. The interface should help buyers understand what a listing contains, who made it, and what happens after purchase.

## Visual direction

- Keep the existing cube mark and recognizable charcoal, orange, and light neutral palette.
- Use the product previews and creator supplied images as the main visual material; do not substitute decorative stock imagery for product contents.
- Use direct Indonesian copy, sentence case, and left alignment for explanatory content.
- Keep one clear primary action per section. Remove labels and badges that repeat information or imply unverified popularity.
- Motion should follow user actions. Do not rotate featured listings automatically or use pointer tilt for static product art. Respect reduced motion.
- At phone widths, preserve readable product titles, visible prices, and tap targets without horizontal overflow.

## Content integrity

- Do not display invented review, sales, popularity, verification, licensing, or update claims.
- Ratings and reviews may appear only when backed by actual review records. A new creator listing starts unrated.
- Example URLs must not be presented as usable product access links.
- Keep the existing catalog and business flows usable while accurately describing listing data and delivery.

## Scope

Audit home, catalog cards, creator listing defaults, and product detail social proof. Refine only visual elements tied to these integrity issues; preserve navigation, marketplace identity, and existing buyer/creator flows.

## Acceptance

- The home page and catalog do not present fabricated rating, sales, bestseller, or testimonial claims.
- Newly published creator products have no fabricated reviews and neutral default descriptions/inclusions.
- Product detail counters and review display use real recorded values or a clear empty state.
- Desktop and phone layouts remain legible and usable.
- Production build succeeds and changes are published to the configured GitHub/Vercel workflow.
